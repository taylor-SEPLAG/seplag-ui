## Prompts utilizados

### banner

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: decorative panoramic Christmas background for a Brazilian government dashboard, NOT a website screenshot, no text or interface. Very wide horizontal winter panorama with light icy blue and white sky, gentle snowdrifts, snow-laden evergreen trees. Preserve the LEFT 38 percent as extremely light open negative space for dark readable live UI text. On the middle-right, a cheerful richly illustrated Santa in a glossy red and gold sleigh full of presents, pulled toward the right by three realistic stylized brown reindeer with red harness and gold bells. A cozy small wooden chalet with glowing amber windows and snowy roof at the far right, fir trees behind. Rich polished three-dimensional Christmas greeting-card art, fine pine needles and fur detail, luminous warm highlights, convincing materials, sophisticated soft shadows, red ribbons at the far corners. Bright airy daytime winter atmosphere, NOT dark green sky, NOT flat vector geometric art. Composition designed to be cropped as a slim 180px tall banner: keep all main subjects across the central horizontal band, small enough to see entire sleigh, antlers and house, avoid any tall foreground subject. Desired extremely panoramic canvas, approximately 2400 by 480. No words, no letters, no logo, no UI, no watermark.

### decor

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ONE square transparent PNG sprite atlas of detailed Christmas decorations for a web dashboard, arranged on an invisible EXACT four-column by four-row grid, same-sized cells, NO grid lines or labels. Entire background genuinely transparent alpha. Rich realistic polished festive 3D Christmas greeting-card illustration, luminous gold reflections, detailed evergreen needles, saturated glossy scarlet ribbon, white snow, not flat vector. ROW 1 across all four cells: a wide evergreen garland with golden fairy lights, pinecones, holly berries and a large central pair of golden Christmas bells beneath a luxurious glossy red bow. The garland occupies just the first quarter of canvas height. ROW 2 (each object centered in its own cell): col1 cluster of red and green wrapped gifts with gold bows and pine sprigs, col2 ornate glowing golden lantern surrounded by holly berries and snowy pine, col3 small diagonal holly corner sprig with dark pointed green leaves and three red berries, col4 single shiny scarlet hanging bauble with delicate white snowflake and golden cap. ROW 3 each cell: col1 golden five-point Christmas star, col2 pair of golden bells with red bow, col3 pinecones with snowy pine branch and red berries, col4 soft red Santa hat with fluffy white trim. ROW 4 across all four cells: low continuous soft white snowy ridge with very pale icy fir silhouettes, leaving the rest of the row transparent. Precisely keep every item inside its designated cell with transparent margins of at least 8 percent; do not overlap rows or neighboring cells. No backdrop shadows filling cells; only soft local contact shadows. No text, no letters, no UI, no border, no checkerboard. Square 2048x2048 atlas.

Fine realistic pine needles and fur, rich glossy Christmas greeting-card illustration. No words, no logo, no letters, no office background, no UI.

### Refinamento final do banner

Edit this generated winter panorama for a VERY SHALLOW WEBSITE BANNER. Preserve the rich detailed polished 3D Christmas greeting-card style, cheerful Santa, red and gold sleigh with presents, three brown reindeer, cozy glowing chalet at far right, snowy firs, light icy sky and snow. Keep canvas approximately 3:1. Crucial composition change: RESCALE ALL MAIN SUBJECTS SMALLER and place them entirely within the CENTRAL HORIZONTAL THIRD of the canvas, between 34% and 66% of the image height. Santa's hat, sleigh runners, reindeer antlers and legs, and chalet roof and base must ALL fit inside that narrow middle band. Make the whole upper third almost empty pale icy-blue sky; lower third almost empty white snow. The left 38% of canvas stays very pale nearly white unobstructed negative space for live UI copy. Sleigh begins at x40%, three reindeer spread across x55%-83%, small chalet at x92%. Keep reindeer and Santa anatomically proportional, simply shrink and reposition them rather than squashing. A few small snowy firs along the horizon within that central strip. Remove large corner bows or foreground objects that extend outside the central band. No text, no UI, no labels, no watermark. This image will be cropped to the middle strip, so all key subject silhouettes must remain within that middle third.
